Let's say that our Env Var address is 0xffffd8c2
we'll add ~16 to it, cause it's secure
0xffffd8c2 + 16 = 0xffffd8d2

Then we have the destiny GOT de exit = 0x80497e0

Dirección GOT	    Byte a escribir	    En decimal
0x80497e0	            0xd2	            199
0x80497e1	            0xd8	            216
0x80497e2	            0xff	            255
0x80497e3	            0xff	            255

We start by entering the 4 destination addresses (16 bytes). 
Then fill in the rest with %Ni to increment the counter to the 
value immediately before each write:
There are 16 characters (the addresses).

To reach 210: 210 - 16 = 194 remains → %194i, then write the byte 0xc7.

To reach 216: you need 216 - 210 = 6 → %6i, then write 0xd8.

To reach 255: you need 255 - 216 = 39 → %39i, then write 0xff (twice, in bytes 2 and 3).
